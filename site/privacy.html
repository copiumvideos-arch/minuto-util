Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers the Minuto Útil publishing tool, which uploads the Minuto Útil channel's own history videos, and this website.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the owner of the Minuto Útil channel, to publish to that channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and never signs in to anyone else's account.

Data it accesses

  • TikTok user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar, to confirm it is the channel's account.
  • TikTok video.upload: to send the channel's videos as drafts to the account's TikTok inbox. The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.
  • Google youtube.upload and youtube.readonly: to upload the channel's videos and to confirm the signed-in channel.

It cannot delete or edit posts, read messages, or access followers' data.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer or in the owner's private build secrets. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Revoking access in the TikTok or Google account settings removes it at any time.
