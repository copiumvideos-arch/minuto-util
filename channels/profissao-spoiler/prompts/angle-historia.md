## Angle

This video is the history of the game: where the idea came from, who made it and how, what went wrong on the way,
how it came out and how it was received, and what it left behind. The facts come in the order they happened, so the
viewer follows one story from start to end. The hook is still the hardest-to-believe fact of that story (a tiny team,
a near cancellation, a record, a decision that changed everything), said first, before the story starts from the
beginning. Test: someone who watched it could retell how the game came to be in three sentences.

## How the facts move

1. Open with the strange fact itself, straight away, at most 20 words.
2. Go back to the beginning in one plain sentence ("Tudo começou em dois mil e onze, quando...") and tell the story
   in order: the studio and the idea, the making (team, budget, technology, delays, cuts), the launch and how it sold
   and was received, then what came after (awards, sequels, updates, records). One fact per section, linked in spoken
   words when one leads to the next ("então", "só que", "aí", "depois disso", "anos depois").
3. When the story reaches the hook's fact, tell it again briefly in its place, with the detail the hook left out.
4. Say where a striking fact comes from when the sources name it: "segundo o diretor", "numa entrevista, ele disse,
   abre aspas, ...". Quoting what a person actually said, word for word from the sources, is welcome.
5. Tell the making, not the plot: the game's own story gets one or two sentences at most, never its ending or a late
   twist.
6. End on the last fact of the story, the most recent one the sources give. The last sentence is a fact, said plainly.
